How the scores work

Every number is relative: 100 means the best combination of three in our data for that measure.

  • University access — how many of the listed university courses your subjects satisfy. Missing a required subject shuts a course; missing a recommended one counts as partly open. The most selective courses count for more.
  • Career prospects — which degree areas are open to you, weighted by graduate outcomes for that area (in the real version: the government's LEO earnings and employment data).
  • Keeps options open — how many degree areas stay open, whatever they lead to.
  • Fits together — of the degrees your subjects specifically point towards, how many you fully qualify for. Stops the tool recommending a random subject just because it opens an unrelated door.
  • Overall — a blend of these, with the first three weighted by Prioritise. If you choose a goal, 40% of the overall score is how well you fit that goal.

With fewer than three subjects, the score shows the best you can still reach (“up to”).

The score can't know what you enjoy or are good at — and grades matter more than combinations. Pick subjects you'll do well in.
