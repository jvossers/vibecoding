How to read a workflow

  1. Lanes are components. Each band is something you can prepare on its own: the pasta, the sauce, the cheese.
  2. Arrows are real prerequisites. An arrow means the next step can't start until the previous one is done. No arrow, no waiting.
  3. Merges show where streams meet. Several arrows into one step means those components come together there.
  4. It isn't a timeline. Left to right means "comes after", not "takes longer". A short lane may still take ages.
