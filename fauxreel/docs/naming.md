# Naming

## Decision: **Faux Reel**

- "Faux" (French for fake) sounds like "fo", so the name reads as **"for real"** (the chats look for real) *and* as **"fake reel"** (a fake video). One name, two jokes, and it describes the product.
- Logo: "faux" in ink, "reel" in pink (Bagel Fat One). Written as `fauxreel` in the wordmark.
- Preferred domain: **fauxreel.app**. Not yet registered or checked at time of writing.
- Considered and rejected: **fo-reel.app** (hyphens are hard to say aloud and people drop them; "fo" is ambiguous) and **foreel.app** (can be misread as "fore-el").
- The app was called **ChatReel** first. The old `/chatreel/` path redirects, and the `localStorage` keys still use `chatreel` so existing users keep their settings.

## Rules

- **Never** put a chat app's brand in the name or domain (WhatsApp, iMessage, Instagram …). Meta and others enforce their trademarks.
- Check domain availability at a registrar before committing to a name. Where affordable, grab the `.com` too.
