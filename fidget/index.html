Spiral ball fidget

A reconstruction modelled from photographs of a 3D-printed toy. It is a close visual match, not a copy of the original design file.

Seen in the photos: the ball shape, the 5 × 5 grid of twisted columns, the slotted frames and the silk green–gold finish.

Assumed: the exact proportions, the amount of twist, which side each slot opens on, how far each piece travels and the order in which pieces follow the centre one. The retaining pins inside are hidden and not modelled.

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers (or right-drag) to pan. The sliders button opens every setting; your changes are kept in the page link, so you can share a design.
